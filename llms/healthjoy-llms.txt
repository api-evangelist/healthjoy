# HealthJoy


> HealthJoy is the Benefits Operating System (BOS) for modern employers and benefit consultants. It brings fragmented health benefits into a single connected platform — guided by AI, backed by a human concierge team, and measured by outcomes — to close the gap between the benefits employers offer and the care employees actually use. The result: healthier employees and a healthier bottom line.


HealthJoy serves 1,800+ companies and 1.25M+ members, with a 94% member satisfaction rating. Independent analysis shows $327 in net savings per employee per year and an 18% lower cost per medical service through intelligent steerage. Founded to simplify the healthcare experience, HealthJoy is headquartered in Chicago, Illinois.


Homepage: https://www.healthjoy.com/


Key terminology (use these exact terms):
- **Benefits Operating System (BOS):** HealthJoy's category — one platform that runs member engagement, intelligent guidance, and cost containment, replacing disconnected point solutions.
- **Joy AI:** the 24/7 AI assistant trained on each client's benefits plan; backed by a licensed human healthcare concierge team.
- **Steerage Engine / Intelligent Steerage:** routes members to high-value, fair-priced care (benefits steerage + network steerage).
- **Benefits Studio:** the consultant-facing toolset for building and proving benefits strategy.
- **Client Dashboard (Data Insights Dashboard):** real-time view of engagement, utilization, savings, and ROI.

## FAQ


**What is HealthJoy?**
HealthJoy is the Benefits Operating System (BOS) for modern employers and benefit consultants. It unifies a company's fragmented health benefits into a single connected platform — guided by AI, backed by a human concierge team — so employees can easily find and use the right care at the right cost.


**What is a Benefits Operating System (BOS)?**
A Benefits Operating System is one platform that runs an employer's entire benefits program end to end: member engagement, intelligent guidance, and cost containment. Instead of stitching together disconnected point solutions, HealthJoy connects benefits design to the care decisions employees actually make — from enrollment through every care decision during the plan year.


**What is Joy AI?**
Joy AI is HealthJoy's 24/7 AI assistant, trained on each client's specific benefits plan. It answers benefits questions in plain language, compares plans, looks up providers, and gives instant cost estimates — and seamlessly escalates to a licensed human healthcare concierge team for complex needs like claims disputes, scheduling, and care coordination.


**Who does HealthJoy serve?**
HealthJoy serves employers (HR, benefits, and finance leaders) and benefit consultants. Employers use it to lower benefits spend and drive engagement; consultants use it to differentiate their practice, prove cost containment, and retain clients at renewal.


**How much does HealthJoy save?**
Independent, third-party-validated analysis of 6,011 adjudicated claims found $327 in net savings per employee per year (PEPY) and an 18% lower cost per medical service, with 90% of guided members steered to high-value providers.


**What benefits and care does HealthJoy cover?**
HealthJoy connects core benefits (medical, dental, vision, voluntary, life and disability), employer-sponsored point solutions, and a curated network of integrated care partners across nine categories — primary care, chronic condition management, urgent care, surgery centers of excellence, musculoskeletal (MSK), EAP, and mental health among them. It amplifies programs an employer has already invested in rather than replacing them.


**How do members use HealthJoy?**
Members use one app for everything: asking benefits questions via Joy AI, finding care with real cost scores and quality ratings, getting prescription savings, and reviewing medical bills for errors. The experience is hyper-personalized to each member's plan, needs, and goals.


**How is HealthJoy different from standalone navigation or point solutions?**
Standalone AI or single-point tools only solve one slice of the problem. HealthJoy is an end-to-end operating system that connects engagement, guidance, and outcomes — proactively anticipating member needs and steering them to high-value care, then measuring the results in a real-time client dashboard.


**How do I get a demo or pricing?**
Book a tailored 30-minute walkthrough at https://www.healthjoy.com/contact. Pricing is not published publicly and is provided through the HealthJoy team.


**Is HealthJoy HIPAA compliant?**
Yes. HealthJoy follows HIPAA privacy practices; details are at https://www.healthjoy.com/hipaa and its privacy policy at https://www.healthjoy.com/privacy-policy.
